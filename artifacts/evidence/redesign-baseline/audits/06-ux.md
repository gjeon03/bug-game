## 0. Headline

**The game boots into its own emergency state and never leaves it during the tutorial.**

```
START_WATER = 34            (constants.ts:101)
WATER_CAP   = 160           (constants.ts:86)
CRITICAL_RESERVE = 0.22     (constants.ts:161)   ->  critical below 35.2
```

34 < 35.2, so `updateObjective`'s shortage branch (`director.ts:356`) wins on the **first simulation
step**. Headless trace, seed 1234, no player input:

```
t=0.00 (pre-step) : Leave the crack and find something to eat.
t=0.02            : MOISTURE RUNNING OUT — run a trail to water now or the colony dies.
t=11.12           : FOOD AND MOISTURE RUNNING OUT — run a trail to water now or the colony dies.
(unchanged through t=90)
```

The project's own probe agrees — `redesign-baseline/playtest-night1-probe.json`:

```
"firstObjective": "MOISTURE RUNNING OUT — run a trail to water now or the colony dies."
decisionLog: t=0.8 shortage · t=11.1 shortage · t=19.9 shortage · t=134.9 "Keep both food and moisture flowing."
```

Four objective changes in a 178 s night; the first non-crisis line lands at **t = 134.9 s (76 % of
night 1)**. `shots/01-boot.png`, `02-onboarding.png`, `05`, `06` and the newest
`redesign-baseline/shots/01-cold-load.png` (2:57 left) and `03-first-route.png` (2:50 left) all show the
same red sentence. It is the first thing a new player reads and it is a lie: nothing is running out,
this is the designed starting state.

Consequence: **`'Link the nest to food or moisture with a pheromone trail.'` — the one objective that
names the core verb — is unreachable during onboarding.** It only appears when reserves are healthy
*and* no route is linked, i.e. after the player has already worked out routing on their own.

## 3. Specific findings requested

**5. Late-game guidance when everything is at cap.** Traced:

```
N3, all nests claimed, food 320/320, water 220/220, pop 52/52
  -> "Hold the kitchen. Survive the final response."     guide arrow -> "Sink drip · 3900 left"
N2, all night-2 cracks claimed, food 200/200, water 160/160
  -> "Build reserves — 200/120 food, 160/90 moisture."   (both targets already beaten)
N2, pop 40, food 130, water 200
  -> "Build reserves — 130/120 food, 200/90 moisture."   AND world.banking === true
```

The last one is the worst state in the game. `colony.ts:85` silently stops all breeding
(`bankingFirst`), the BROOD meter freezes at 0 %, the objective demands reserves the player already
has, and `world.banking` — a dedicated field on the World, `world.ts:140` — **is read by no file in
`src/ui/` or `src/render/`.** The signal was built and never wired to a pixel.

**6. Does the HUD explain why suspicion rose?** Partly. `.cause` shows the last cause label and
`.next` shows the next response (`hud.ts:167-176`), and `09-escalation.png` reads correctly:
*"A roach was seen in the light" / "sticky traps go down on your busiest floor routes."* But:
`lastCause` is **never cleared** (`suspicion.ts:71`) and `lastCauseTime` is written and never read, so
the row shows a stale cause indefinitely; there is no magnitude, no rate, no *"+7 just now"*; the
`.next` string is rendered mid-sentence lowercase because the HUD strips `"Next: "` (`hud.ts:175`)
from a constant that was written to include it; and a tier crossing produces sound + flash + shake but
zero words.

**7. Next-unlock communication.** None, beyond the proximity prompt. Nothing anywhere states that
Brood chamber = +14 capacity ×1.75 hatch rate, Food cache = +120/+60 caps, Escape tunnel = teleport
home. Verified against `constants.ts:83-88` and `colony.ts:34-48`: those numbers exist only in code.

**8. Blocker naming.** When unaffordable, `#prompt` only turns red (`hud.ts:205`, `style.css:378`) —
**the text is byte-identical to the affordable version**. Pressing E yields
`"Brood chamber needs 40 food and 26 moisture."` (`colony.ts:282`), which restates the cost and still
never says what you have or how short you are.

**3. Objective transitions.** `updateObjective` reads only reserves, routes, night, nests, population
and `finalResponse`. It has **no branch for patrols, sprays, traps, bait, scout dead, or scout
trapped**. `shots/09-escalation.png`: scout is dead ("replacement in 0.4s") and the objective says
*"run a trail to water now"* — an action the player cannot perform. A tier-4 spray cloud sitting on
the home crack does not change the objective at all.

**4. Unreadable markers / objective-arrow disagreement.** `updateGuide` (`director.ts:301`) picks its
target from a **different priority chain** than the objective text. Verified with both a food and a
water line live:

```
N2, food 60 < water 100, crack unclaimed -> "Claim Brood chamber (…)"  arrow -> Dishwasher crumbs   NO
N2, food 100 > water 60,  crack unclaimed -> "Claim Brood chamber (…)"  arrow -> Brood chamber       yes
N3, all cracks, pop 20                    -> "Reach 36 roaches — 20 so far."  arrow -> Sink drip     NO
N3, FINAL RESPONSE                        -> "FINAL RESPONSE — keep the colony alive."  arrow -> Sink drip  NO
```

**Three of four realistic states point the player somewhere other than where the words send them** —
including during the extermination sweep, where the arrow calmly recommends a water source.

Other marker problems: the PHEROMONE meter renders `100 · 0/5` under one label
(`hud.ts:135`) — two different quantities (reserve, live routes) sharing a caption, and `0/5` is never
explained until the eviction toast fires. BROOD is a bare `%` with no statement of what it counts
down to. The guide chevron and the exposure-warning arc are both `PAL.warm`/`PAL.danger` rings
(`renderer.ts:1101`, `1288`) — same visual vocabulary for "go here" and "you are being seen".

**1./2. Hidden prerequisites.** Never stated anywhere in the UI: the 5-route cap (only after it
evicts your route); that routes need *both* ends within `LINK_RADIUS = 92`; that worker traffic
refreshes trail nodes so a used route sustains itself; that a fully-drained food source permanently
raises suspicion; that resources regrow between nights but drained ones regrow less
(`director.ts:180`); that suspicion has a floor that ratchets with peak.

**Bottom-centre stacking.** `hud.ts:180-208` arbitrates toast/prompt/tutorial into one slot — but
`#objective` is a *fourth*, separately mounted element in the same `.bc` column (`hud.ts:68-73`), so
two lines still stack. `05-workers-hauling.png` shows prompt + objective; `09-escalation.png` shows
tutorial + objective, with the tutorial stuck on `E — inspect the crumbs` 94 s into the night because
step `inspect` has no timeout (`onboarding.ts:31-35` — every other step has a time-based escape).

## 5. Proposed redesign

### 5.1 Objective hierarchy — four slots, not one line

Replace the single `#objective` string with a four-row stack rendered bottom-centre, each row
independently present or absent, always in this order. Each row answers a different question and none
of them may restate a meter.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ① OPERATION   what I am doing, and where          (always)     │
│ ② BLOCKER     what is stopping me, and the gap    (when true)  │
│ ③ THREAT      what is coming, and the counter     (when true)  │
│ ④ HORIZON     next unlock / win progress          (always)     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Row ② takes visual priority (danger colour) but **never replaces** ①, so the player never loses the
thread. Row ③ pre-empts ② only while a lethal thing is on screen.

### 5.2 String templates

**① Operation** — must name a *verb*, a *place* and a *why*. Place comes from the same object the
guide chevron points at, so D3 is fixed by construction: `world.guide` becomes the single source and
the operation string is generated *from it*.

```
Run a moisture line to {guide.label} — {distance} tiles {bearing}. Moisture feeds brood.
Claim {nest.label} at {distance} tiles — unlocks {benefit}.
Hold {n} lines while the larder fills.                        (banking state)
Pull everyone off the floor — the sweep starts in {t}s.       (final response)
```

`{benefit}` is authored data on `NestSpec`, not derived: `"+14 roaches, brood hatches 1.75× faster"` /
`"+120 food, +60 moisture storage"` / `"instant retreat to the home crack"`. Fixes D9.

**② Blocker** — only ever rendered when something is actually refused, and it must state the **gap**,
not the cost:

```
Blocked: {nest.label} needs {need_food - have_food} more food. ({have}/{need})
Blocked: all 5 trails in use — rub one out (RMB) before laying a new one.
Blocked: {resource.label} is stripped bare. Nearest {kind} is {other.label}, {n} tiles {bearing}.
Blocked: colony is at capacity {pop}/{cap} — claim a crack to raise it.
Paused: breeding held while the larder fills — {have}/{need} food, {have}/{need} moisture.
```

That last one is D6: surface `world.banking` in words. Fixes D5, D6, D10.

**③ Threat** — a named threat and a named counter-play, replacing today's silent flash:

```
Sticky traps went down on your busiest line — reroute or rub it out.
Torch sweeping the {area}. Recall (tap RMB) or wait it out in cover.
Spray heading for the home crack — {t}s. Get bodies off the floor.
You were seen. Suspicion +{n} ({cause}). Stay under the cabinets to let it settle.
```

Triggered off the existing `{t:'tier'}` / `trapArmed` / `sprayStart` events, which already fire and
today only produce noise. Fixes D8, D12.

**④ Horizon** — a persistent one-liner that makes the win legible from minute one:

```
Night 1/3 · 36 roaches, 120 food, 90 moisture, 3 chambers, survive the sweep
Night 2/3 · roaches 18/36 · food 74/120 · moisture 91/90 ✓ · chambers 1/3
Night 3/3 · 4 of 5 met — {missing} is the only thing left
```

Drive it from `winProgress()`, which already exists and is dead. Fixes D7. Night 1 shows the
criteria; nights 2–3 show progress against them. Met criteria get a tick and stop being restated.

### 5.3 The shortage state

Raise the trigger so it cannot be the boot state, and split "low" from "critical":

- `START_WATER` and `START_FOOD` must sit above `CRITICAL_RESERVE × cap` with real headroom
  (water needs ≥ ~48 against `WATER_CAP 160`), **or** suppress the shortage branch until the first
  delivery has landed (`stats.firstDeliveryAt >= 0`). Fixes D1, D2.
- Two tiers instead of one: **low** (amber, row ①, *"Moisture is thin — the sink drip is 3 tiles
  north"*) and **critical** (red, row ②, *"Moisture out in ~{t}s at current upkeep"*). A projected
  time-to-empty is honest guidance; a static red sentence for 135 s is not.
- When both are critical, name both remedies in priority order, not one arbitrarily. Fixes D11.

### 5.4 World-guidance affordances

1. **One arrow, one objective.** `updateGuide` becomes a consumer of the operation row, not a
   parallel policy. If the words say "claim the brood chamber", the chevron points at the brood
   chamber. (D3)
2. **Distinct vocabularies.** Guide = warm dashed ring + chevron. Danger = red solid arc. Today both
   are pulsing rings in adjacent hues (`renderer.ts:1101` vs `1288`). Give the guide a chevron-only
   on-screen form so a ring always means *threat*. (D4)
3. **Persistent hazard markers.** Armed traps and bait get a low-opacity floor decal for as long as
   they are live, not a one-frame particle ring. A trap the player cannot see is not a decision.
4. **Sealed cracks are visible from range.** Draw a dim locked-crack glyph with `night {n}` at map
   scale so the three expansion sites are legible without a 104-unit walk. (D13)
5. **Route health at the meter.** Split `100 · 0/5` into `PHEROMONE 100` and a separate
   `LINES 3/5` row, each line dotted with its resource kind and a dry/live state. (D17)
6. **Capacity/cap changes announced.** On claim, toast `Brood chamber built — capacity 14 → 36,
   hatching 1.75×`. Currently the only feedback is two nymphs spawning. (D9)

### 5.5 Onboarding

- Every step gets a time escape, as 6 of 8 already have; `inspect` and `lay` must not be able to pin
  the tutorial for 94 s. (D14)
- Move the framing sentence out of the ESC menu into step 0 of the tutorial:
  *"You are one scout. The colony only follows trails you walked yourself."* (D18)
- Fold `#objective` into the same arbitration group as toast/prompt/tutorial so bottom-centre can
  never stack two competing lines; the four objective rows are one block that yields to a toast. (D15)
- Teach the unstated rules where they bite: route cap on the 4th route (not the 6th), link radius the
  first time a lay ends short, depletion penalty the first time a source runs dry. (D19)

### 5.6 Cleanup implied

`director.ts:116` `{t:'objective'}` — either handle it or delete it (D16). `winProgress()` and
`tierLabel()` gain their first call sites under §5.2 ④. `suspicion.lastCauseTime` should drive an
expiry on the `.cause` row (fade after ~12 s to *"Nothing new."*), and `TIER_RESPONSE` should drop its
`"Next: "` prefix rather than have the HUD regex it away.
