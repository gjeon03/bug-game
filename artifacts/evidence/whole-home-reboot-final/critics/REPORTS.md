# Independent critic reports

Five fresh-context critics reviewed the build against `CLAUDE.md` section 10. They were told the
author's own `GAUNTLET_STATE.md` is a CLAIM to be checked, not truth. Their verdicts are recorded
here verbatim, including the ones that are still open.

## Korean UX critic (native speaker) — **FAIL**

The catalog is genuinely well-written Korean — tense, idiomatic, consistent 해라체, correct keep-all line breaking, zero tofu at all four tested viewports, zero missing keys, and no player-facing English anywhere. The author's own §4a rule ("particles are computed, never hardcoded") is the thing the build violates: four keys hardcode the written-form fallback `(으)로`, and it renders literally on screen. `음식물 쓰레기통(으)로 가는 페로몬 길을 놓았다.` is visible in three of the six final evidence screenshots (05, 06, 07) and is the FIRST Korean sentence the game ever writes — every route, every gate, every alert escalation. That alone is a ship blocker. Underneath it, a second grammar class fails: `log.threat.incoming` renders `주방에 불이 켜짐이 온다.` and `주방에 행주질이 온다.` for 4 of 7 threats. And structurally, 51 authored player-facing keys — including all 7 route-diagnostic strings, the WebGL2 error, the loading screen, and the adaptation cost — are never rendered by any code path. The author's claim that untranslated text is "structurally impossible" (hud.ts:12-15) is true for *missing* translation and false for *wrong* Korean, and there is not one i18n test in the repo to catch either.

### [BLOCKER] `(으)로` renders literally on screen — the game's first Korean sentence is broken
- **evidence:** src/i18n/ko.ts:169, 224, 231, 236 hardcode the written-form fallback `(으)로`. Rendered through the real t(): `음식물 쓰레기통(으)로 가는 페로몬 길을 놓았다.` / `거실(으)로 가는 길이 열렸다.` / `거실 경계 단계가 경계(으)로 올라갔다.` / `싱크대 밑 공간(으)로 들어가는 보급선이 끊겨 있다`. Visible on screen in artifacts/evidence/whole-home-reboot-final/05-colony-working.png (bottom right, lines 1 and 3), 06-1280x720.png and 07-1440x900.png.
- **why it costs the player:** log.route.laid fires the moment the player draws their first pheromone route — inside the first ~20 seconds of every run, and again on every route for the whole game. `(으)로` is form-filling boilerplate a Korean reader associates with a 관공서 document, never with prose. Seeing it inside a narrative sentence reads instantly as unfinished software, and it is the very first sentence the game writes. It also directly contradicts the file's own header rule at ko.ts:10-11 and CLAUDE.md §4a.
- **suggested fix:** This needs a THREE-way rule, not the existing two-way helper: 로 after a vowel, 로 after ㄹ 받침, 으로 otherwise. `endsWithConsonant()` (index.ts:56) cannot express it — it returns true for 물 and would produce `샤워 트레이 고인 물으로`. Add a distinct `{x?으로/로}`-style resolver that special-cases jongseong index 8 (ㄹ), then write `{target}{target?으로/로}` in the four keys. Verify against 거실/욕실/침실 (all ㄹ-final → 로) and 주방 (→ 으로).

### [HIGH] log.threat.incoming produces ungrammatical Korean for 4 of the 7 threats
- **evidence:** ko.ts:238 `'{region}에 {threat}{threat?이/가} 온다.'` combined with the seven labelKeys at src/colony/household.ts:72-132. Rendered output: `주방에 불이 켜짐이 온다.` · `주방에 행주질이 온다.` · `주방에 물건 치우기가 온다.` · `주방에 발소리가 온다.` Only 끈끈이 / 로봇청소기 / 살충제 produce natural sentences.
- **why it costs the player:** This is the `danger` severity line (household.ts:334) — the one the player must parse in under a second while deciding whether to run. `불이 켜짐이 온다` is not a sentence a Korean speaker can produce; a light being switched on does not 'come'. The template assumes every threat name is a physical object, but four of seven are 동사의 명사형. The player loses the one line that is supposed to buy them reaction time.
- **suggested fix:** Split the template by threat type. Give each threat a full predicate key instead of a bare noun — e.g. threat.light → `주방에 불이 켜진다`, threat.wipe → `주방을 행주로 닦는다`, threat.move → `거실 물건을 치우기 시작한다`, threat.footsteps → `주방 쪽으로 발소리가 다가온다` — and keep `{region}에 {threat}{threat?이/가} 온다` only for the three object threats, or drop it entirely.

### [HIGH] WebGL2 failure shows the wrong Korean and tells the player to press a key that does nothing
- **evidence:** src/game/boot.ts:384-386 renders only `t('error.runtime')` = '문제가 생겼다. R을 눌러 다시 시작해라.' There is no WebGL2 capability check anywhere in src (grep for webgl in boot.ts returns only comments). ko.ts:303 `'error.webgl': '이 브라우저에서는 3D 화면을 켤 수 없다. WebGL2를 켜고 다시 열어라.'` is never referenced by any file. Ordering: createRenderer is at boot.ts:88, requestAnimationFrame(tick) is at boot.ts:324 — so a renderer construction failure means the frame loop that drains the input queue never starts, and R is inert.
- **why it costs the player:** A player on a machine without WebGL2 gets a message that misdiagnoses the problem AND instructs an action that cannot work. They press R, nothing happens, they press it again, they leave. The correct sentence — which tells them exactly what to do — was written and then orphaned. This is the failure path where clear Korean matters most, because the player has no other information.
- **suggested fix:** Probe `canvas.getContext('webgl2')` before constructing the renderer and render `t('error.webgl')` on null. Separately, drop 'R을 눌러 다시 시작해라' from error.runtime unless the input loop is provably live at that point — say '새로 고침해라' instead.

### [HIGH] All seven route-health strings are authored and never shown — the player is never told why a route failed
- **evidence:** ko.ts:260-266 defines hud.routeHealth.ok / incomplete / disconnected / blocked / congested / compromised / washed ('양 끝이 붙지 않았다', '끊김', '먹이가 떨어졌다', '통로가 막혔다', '들킨 길', '쓸려 나갔다'). `grep -rn 'routeHealth' src --exclude ko.ts` returns 0 hits. hud.ts has no route panel at all; the HUD is objective / stores / regions / prompt / log only (hud.ts:94-101). `hud.routes` ('페로몬 길', ko.ts:252) is likewise dead.
- **why it costs the player:** CLAUDE.md §10 names pheromone logistics as THE differentiator. The author wrote six distinct Korean diagnoses for six distinct route failure modes and then shipped none of them, so a route that stops delivering is silent — the player sees workers stop and has no way to distinguish '양 끝이 붙지 않았다' (my fault, redraw it) from '쓸려 나갔다' (not my fault, wait). That is the difference between a system you learn and a system that feels random.
- **suggested fix:** Add a routes panel to hud.ts rendering one row per active route with `t(`hud.routeHealth.${route.health}`)`, or at minimum surface the non-ok states as log entries. The strings already exist and read well; only the render path is missing.

### [MEDIUM] Final-chapter blocker numbers are hardcoded inside the Korean strings and duplicate the sim constants
- **evidence:** ko.ts:174 `'blocker.population': '군체가 더 커져야 한다 — 일꾼 12마리'` and ko.ts:175 `'blocker.stores': '비축이 모자라다 — 먹이 30, 수분 20'` duplicate the literals at src/colony/step.ts:154 (`population < 12`) and step.ts:155 (`food < 30 || moisture < 20`). Every other blocker takes live params: ko.ts:165-167 use `{have}/{need}` (rendered as '일꾼이 부족하다 — 3/7' in 05/06/07).
- **why it costs the player:** Two costs. First, if anyone retunes step.ts the Korean silently lies to the player about the win condition. Second and worse for the player: these are the last two gates before victory, and they are the only two blockers in the game with no live progress readout — the player is told '일꾼 12마리' with no idea whether they are at 4 or at 11, in the exact phase where CLAUDE.md §10 forbids an unexplained decision plateau over 45 s.
- **suggested fix:** Export the thresholds from step.ts, pass `{have}/{need}` like every other blocker, and rewrite as '군체가 더 커져야 한다 — {have}/{need}' / '비축이 모자라다 — 먹이 {food}/{foodNeed}, 수분 {moisture}/{moistureNeed}'.

### [MEDIUM] Adaptation cards show no cost and no remaining points
- **evidence:** src/colony/progression.ts:236 computes `costKey: `adaptation.${family}.${tier}.cost``, but hud.ts:244-255 renderChoices renders only `offer.labelKey` and `offer.bodyKey` — costKey is dropped. The six '적응 1' strings (ko.ts:180, 183, 186, 189, 192, 195) are therefore unreachable, and `hud.adaptationPoints` ('쓸 수 있는 적응 {count}', ko.ts:257) is referenced nowhere in src.

CLAUDE.md §10 requires 'two adaptation paths produce observably different runs' — a choice screen that hides its own currency makes the branch feel arbitrary rather than strategic, and there is no Korean text on screen telling them the decision is permanent.

### [MEDIUM] The region panel's middle bar has no label in any language
- **evidence:** hud.ts:226-231 renders `<span class="name">` (주방) + `<div class="heat">` (bare bar) + `<span class="level">` (조용함). The heat bar is `state.evidence` and has no caption. Visible bottom-left in 01, 02, 05, 06, 07 as five rows of 주방/복도/거실/욕실/침실 with an unlabeled bar. ko.ts:253 `'hud.alert': '경계 단계'` and ko.ts:255 `'hud.blocked': '막힌 이유'` and ko.ts:256 `'hud.objective': '지금 할 일'` are all unreferenced in src.

CLAUDE.md §10 requires objectives understandable without documentation; an unlabeled bar next to '조용함' is more likely read as a progress bar for the region than as accumulated evidence. ### [MEDIUM] Household routine names are 명사화 stacks that produce stiff or wrong sentences
- **evidence:** ko.ts:198-203 feed ko.ts:237 `'{routine}{routine?이/가} 곧 시작된다.'`. Rendered: `머리맡 휴대폰이 곧 시작된다.` · `복도 통행이 곧 시작된다.` · `욕실 사용이 곧 시작된다.` · `TV 시청이 곧 시작된다.` Only 설거지가 / 늦은 식사가 read naturally.
- **why it costs the player:** '머리맡 휴대폰이 곧 시작된다' is ungrammatical — a phone does not begin. '복도 통행' and '욕실 사용' are the 한자어 명사 나열 register of a building notice, not of a tense night-time game, and CLAUDE.md §4 explicitly bans long noun stacks. These fire on the `warn` channel to warn the player a human is about to move; stiff bureaucratic Korean blunts the urgency the line exists to create.
- **suggested fix:** Make the routine labels verb phrases and change the template to '{routine}. 곧 시작된다.' — 설거지를 시작한다 / 늦은 식사 / TV를 켠다 / 욕실에 들어간다 / 머리맡에서 휴대폰을 본다 / 복도를 지나간다. Or keep the nouns and change the predicate to '곧 있다'.

### [MEDIUM] Terminology drift: the control list uses '통로 작업' but the world text uses '갉다', and CLAUDE.md's glossary terms 노출 and 대피 appear nowhere
- **evidence:** ko.ts:275 `'help.interact': 'E — 거점 차지 · 통로 작업'` vs the gate descriptions that describe the same action as gnawing: '이걸 갉아 내면' (ko.ts:138), '갉아 내야 한다' (ko.ts:141), '문풍지를 갉는 동안' (ko.ts:150). Separately, CLAUDE.md §4's governed glossary lists 노출 and 대피; grep finds neither anywhere in ko.ts. The HUD uses '들킬 위험' (ko.ts:258) for exposure instead. ko.ts:14-15 rewrites the glossary in-file, silently dropping 노출 and 대피 and adding 정찰병 · 일꾼 · 통로.
- **why it costs the player:** '통로 작업' is the vaguest string in the help card and describes the single most important verb in the game. A first-time player reading the control list has no idea it means gnawing through a seal; they learn it only by walking into a gate. The glossary divergence is a contract-compliance problem, not a quality one — I want to be explicit that '들킬 위험' is BETTER Korean than '노출' for a HUD label, and I would keep it. The defect is that the governing document was edited to match the code rather than the reverse, so the glossary no longer governs anything.
- **suggested fix:** Change help.interact to 'E — 거점 차지 · 통로 갉기'. Then either update CLAUDE.md §4's glossary deliberately (recording that 들킬 위험 replaces 노출 and that 대피 is unimplemented) or restore the terms — but do not leave the two documents disagreeing.

### [MEDIUM] There is no i18n test of any kind, despite the source claiming one exists
- **evidence:** src/i18n/index.ts:26-28: 'Exposed so a unit test can assert it is empty after exercising every screen, which is the only way a missing string gets caught before a player sees a raw key.' tests/unit contains house.test.ts, occlusion.test.ts, profiler.test.ts, run.test.ts — `grep -rn 'MISSING_KEYS\|i18n\|locale' tests scripts` returns exactly one hit, a console.log label in scripts/capture.mjs:78. hud.ts:12-15 makes a stronger claim: 'there is no string in the simulation that could reach a player untranslated… which is the defect this project shipped once before'.
- **why it costs the player:** The structural guarantee the author is proud of covers the wrong failure. CLAUDE.md §4 records that the previous build shipped '2 tiles' and that a real browser caught it in thirty seconds — the lesson written down was 'the locale test must scan rendered output, not catalog contents', and no such test was written. Every finding above (the four `(으)로`, the 4/7 broken threat lines, the 51 orphaned keys) is the kind of thing a rendered-output scan would catch mechanically.
- **suggested fix:** Add a test that (a) asserts MISSING_KEYS is empty after driving the bot through a full run, (b) asserts no rendered HUD string contains `(으)` / `(이)` / `(을)` / `(는)`, and (c) asserts every key in ko.ts is reachable from some code path — that last one alone surfaces all 51 dead keys.

### [LOW] Loading and audio-blocked screens are never shown; index.html duplicates four catalog strings
- **evidence:** ko.ts:301-302 loading.title ('집이 잠들기를 기다리는 중') and loading.assets ('자리를 잡는 중…') and ko.ts:305 error.audioBlocked are unreferenced in src. ### [LOW] Two words for the same concept, and one awkward predicate
- **evidence:** ko.ts:165-167 use 부족하다 ('일꾼이 부족하다', '먹이가 부족하다', '수분이 부족하다') while ko.ts:175 uses 모자라다 ('비축이 모자라다') for the identical shortfall concept. ko.ts:259 `'hud.stores': '비축이 한계다 — 거점을 늘려라'`.
- **why it costs the player:** Minor, but '비축이 한계다' is the one string in the catalog that reads translated — 한계다 as a predicate on 비축 is clipped in a way a native writer would not produce, and it appears at the moment the player is capped and most needs to understand why. The 부족하다/모자라다 split makes two blockers look like different mechanics.
- **suggested fix:** Standardise on 부족하다 for shortfall. Rewrite hud.stores as '비축이 꽉 찼다 — 거점을 늘려라' or '더 쌓을 곳이 없다 — 거점을 늘려라'.

### [BLOCKER] The kitchen is unrecognisable as a kitchen — only the HUD label says otherwise
- **evidence:** 02/04/09-*.png (identical framing) contain: floor, one unbroken black mass, two flat gray quads, ~25 cream cubes/spheres, a blue disc, and one unidentifiable white-and-blue slab. Not one of the five silhouettes kitchen.ts:22-28 names as the room's identity is visible — no worktop horizontal, no fridge box (only a black shape I cannot classify), no rice-cooker drum, no tap gooseneck, no readable 100 mm toe-kick slot. ### [BLOCKER] Half the frame is black; the build is measurably worse than the defect its own comments cite
- **evidence:** 03-kitchen-moved.png and 05-colony-working.png: 48.0% of pixels sit below luminance 0.04. The widest ±6% luminance band holds 61.2% of the frame, centred at 15/255. ### [HIGH] Every occluder compiles a new shader program the first time it fades, mid-play
- **evidence:** occlusion.ts:279-284 toggles `m.alphaHash` false→true and sets `m.needsUpdate = true` on the first frame of every fade. three.module.js:7545 `const HAS_ALPHAHASH = !! material.alphaHash;` and :7639 `alphaHash: HAS_ALPHAHASH` make it a program parameter; :6755 and :6960 emit `#define USE_ALPHAHASH` into both shader stages. So the alphaHash-on variant is a distinct program that does not exist until the fade starts. occlusion.ts:141-144 clones materials per registered group, and my live probe reports 94 registered occluders, so the compile happens once per prop rather than once per material library. occlusion.ts:283 also re-sets `needsUpdate = true` on every frame of the ~13-frame ramp, forcing getParameters + cache-key derivation per material per frame for the whole transition.
- **why it costs the player:** CLAUDE.md §10 requires 'zero shader-compilation stalls during validated active play' and 'no unexplained frame > 100 ms after load'. This guarantees up to 94 of them, spread across the run, each one landing precisely when the player's attention is on the prop that just started fading. GAUNTLET_STATE §4C admits performance is unmeasured on real hardware — this is the specific thing that measurement will find.
- **suggested fix:** Warm the variant at load: after buildScene, set alphaHash = true on every registered occluder material, call renderer.compile(scene, camera), then set it back. Or simpler — leave alphaHash permanently true on occluder materials (opacity 1 makes the hash test pass for every fragment) and drop the toggle entirely, so needsUpdate is only needed when opacity changes, which does not change the program.

### [MEDIUM] Occlusion probe volume is smaller than the scout it is protecting

**What the critic judged good:**
- occlusion.ts:1-35 — choosing Material.alphaHash over transparent/opacity is the right call and the rationale is correct: the material stays in the opaque pass, keeps writing depth, needs no sorting. This designs out the entire alpha-sorting defect class the contract forbids. Verified: three.module.js:7545/7639 confirms alphaHash is a real program parameter, not a no-op.
- occlusion.ts:287 — a faded occluder stops casting shadows. This is the detail almost everyone misses and it is the difference between a fade reading as an affordance and reading as a bug.
- camera.ts:162 — `k = 1 - Math.exp(-dt / FOLLOW_TAU)` instead of a per-frame lerp constant. The follow behaves identically at 60 Hz and 144 Hz, which is what makes a 'camera lag hid the threat' report reproducible at all.
- camera.ts:56,165 — HEIGHT_TAU 0.30 s separate from FOLLOW_TAU 0.16 s. Vertical transitions are eased roughly twice as hard as horizontal ones, and scout.ts:209 lerps climb Y linearly over link.seconds*0.55 (1.32 s for the 880 mm counter cable), so climbs genuinely do not whip the view.
- occlusion.ts:258 — `raycaster.ray.intersectsSphere(o.bounds)` before the recursive mesh test, with bounds computed once at registration. Measured live: 94 registered occluders and the frame cost stays bounded. The cost model is right even though the region filter has a hole in it.
- scene.ts:217-220 — only wall panels get `userData.cameraCollide = true`; props fade instead of shoving the viewpoint. A camera that lurched every time the scout walked past a cupboard would be worse than one that can see through it. Correct call.
- render.ts:212-226 and boot.ts:360-372 — probeView() and the camera readout are real instrumentation, exposed deliberately for evidence capture. They are the reason this review has numbers in it instead of adjectives.
